Your messages stay private

  • Everything you send is scrambled so only the person you're talking to can read it — not us, not anyone in between.
  • Molepipe travels over the Tor network, which hides where you are and who you're talking to.
  • You don't need a phone number or email. Nobody has to know it's you.

    How people reach you

    • Instead of a username, you have a private account number — a long code that's yours alone.
    • Share it with someone you trust, in person or by showing them your QR code, and they can add you.
    • You'll always find your number and QR code under the You tab.

    Locking your app

    • Add a short PIN so you don't have to type your full password every time.
    • Set a duress code: if someone forces you to open the app, it shows an empty decoy and quietly wipes your real data.
    • You can set both up now or later under You › Security.

    Staying in the loop

    • Turn on notifications under the You tab so you know when a message arrives.
    • Your recovery phrase is your only backup — if you lose this device, it's how you get back in. Keep it safe.

    Add a quick PIN?

    A PIN is an optional shortcut to unlock this device — faster than typing your full password. Your password still protects your account; the PIN never leaves this device.

      Set a Duress Code

      A duress code is a fake password. When entered at the unlock screen, it opens a decoy account and silently wipes your real data.

      Erase after failed unlocks
      Off — wrong passwords are only slowed down

      When this is on, this device erases your identity, messages and Tor key after too many failed unlocks in a row — the same as "Remove account". Wrong passwords are always slowed by an increasing delay; this adds the erasure at the end. Any failed unlock counts, so pick a number you won't reach by accident.

      You can always restore your account on any device using your 24-word recovery phrase.
